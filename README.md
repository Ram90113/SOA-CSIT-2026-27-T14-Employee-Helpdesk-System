## Abstract

Human resource management plays a crucial role in organizations by
supporting employees with various workplace-related services, including
recruitment, onboarding, payroll, leave management, attendance,
benefits, and general employee queries. In many organizations, employees
still depend on emails, phone calls, or direct communication with HR
personnel to resolve their concerns. Managing these requests manually
can be time-consuming, difficult to track, and may result in delayed
responses or unresolved issues. These challenges highlight the need for
an efficient and centralized Employee Helpdesk System that can simplify
communication between employees and the HR department.

To address this need, the proposed Recruitment & HR: Employee Helpdesk
System provides a centralized platform through which employees can
submit HR-related queries and service requests. The system allows
employees to raise tickets by selecting appropriate categories such as
recruitment, onboarding, leave, payroll, attendance, benefits, and other
HR-related concerns. Each request is assigned a unique ticket and can be
tracked throughout its lifecycle. HR personnel can view, manage,
prioritize, and respond to employee requests, thereby improving the
efficiency and transparency of the support process.

The system follows a Service-Oriented Architecture (SOA) approach by
dividing the application into independent and reusable services.
Separate services can be implemented for employee management, ticket
management, HR support, authentication, and notifications. These
services communicate with each other through well-defined interfaces,
allowing individual components to be developed, maintained, and modified
independently. The architecture improves scalability, reusability, and
maintainability while reducing the dependency between different modules
of the application.

The proposed system also provides status tracking and notifications to
keep employees informed about the progress of their requests. HR
administrators can update ticket statuses, assign requests to
appropriate personnel, and maintain records of resolved and pending
issues. This centralized management reduces manual effort, improves
response time, and provides a structured mechanism for handling employee
concerns.

Collectively, the Employee Helpdesk System provides an efficient,
scalable, and user-friendly solution for managing HR-related employee
requests. By applying SOA principles and separating the system into
independent services, the proposed solution improves communication,
request tracking, service management, and overall HR support efficiency.
The system can help organizations provide faster and more transparent
assistance to employees while creating a flexible foundation for
integrating additional HR services in the future.
